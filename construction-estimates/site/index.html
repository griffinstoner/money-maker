How it works

Forward the bid invite. Get back a bid you can send.

  1. 1

    Send the plans

    Forward the invite or share a link to the drawings, specs, addenda and bid date. BuildingConnected, plan rooms, Dropbox and email all work.

  2. 2

    We read and measure

    We read your trade's spec sections, list what's included and excluded, flag conflicts between drawings and specs, and do the full quantity takeoff.

  3. 3

    We price it your way

    We load the quantities into your estimate template with your unit prices, waste factors and markup, then check totals against $/SF benchmarks.

  4. 4

    You review and bid

    You get the estimate, marked-up PDF drawings, a scope and exclusions sheet, and a list of open questions to send to the GC.

FAQ

Common questions

What do you need from me?

A link to the drawings, specs and addenda, plus the bid due date. For priced estimates we also need your unit prices or a recent estimate we can use as a template. We set this up once, then reuse it for every job.

Do you set my labor rates or markup?

No. We measure quantities and apply your unit costs, waste factors and markup. You know your crews and your market. We do the measuring so you can focus on pricing and winning the work.

How do you make sure the takeoff is accurate?

Every takeoff goes through a written checklist before it leaves: scale checked on every sheet, every wall type accounted for, quantities cross-checked against $/SF and quantity-per-SF benchmarks. You also get marked-up drawings so you can see exactly what was counted.

What if you miss something?

Tell us and we'll fix it and send it again at no charge, as fast as we can. You always review the estimate before bidding. We're your estimating department, but the bid you submit is yours.

What's the turnaround?

48 hours from receiving a complete plan set for typical projects. Large projects or packages with many addenda get a confirmed date before we start. Send plans at least 3 business days before the bid is due.

Is my pricing kept private?

Yes. Your unit prices, estimates and bid results are never shared with anyone, including other clients.

Is there a contract?

Monthly plans are month-to-month. Cancel with 30 days' notice. Unused takeoffs don't roll over.
