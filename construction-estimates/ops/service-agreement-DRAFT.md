# Estimating Services Agreement: DRAFT

> **⚠️ Draft for discussion only. Not legal advice.** Have a lawyer licensed in your state review this before you use it. The limitation-of-liability and scope sections matter most.

---

This Estimating Services Agreement ("Agreement") is between **[Your legal business name]** ("Provider") and **[Client legal name]** ("Client"), effective **[date]**.

## 1. Services
Provider will prepare quantity takeoffs and estimates ("Deliverables") from drawings, specifications and addenda supplied by Client, for the trade(s) of **[trade]**, under the plan selected below.

- ☐ **Single Takeoff:** $[amount] per project, quoted before work starts.
- ☐ **Bid Desk:** $1,500/month, up to 6 takeoffs per calendar month.
- ☐ **Bid Desk Pro:** $3,000/month, up to 15 takeoffs per calendar month, plus bid sourcing and bid-day support.

Unused takeoffs do not roll over. Projects over **[50,000] SF** of building area, or with unusual scope, may count as more than one takeoff. Provider will tell Client before starting.

## 2. Turnaround
Standard turnaround is 48 hours (2 business days) from receipt of a **complete** document set. Documents received fewer than 3 business days before a bid deadline are handled on a best-effort basis.

## 3. Client responsibilities
Client will provide complete, current documents including all addenda, its unit prices, waste factors, overhead and profit, and will notify Provider of any scope instructions.

## 4. Nature of Deliverables; Client review
Deliverables are estimating aids prepared from the documents provided. **Client is solely responsible for reviewing Deliverables, for all pricing, and for the content and submission of any bid.** Provider does not set labor or material pricing, does not guarantee any bid outcome, and is not responsible for errors in, or changes to, the documents provided. Client should verify quantities it relies on.

## 5. Corrections
If Client reports an error in a Deliverable, Provider will correct and reissue it at no charge as soon as reasonably possible.

## 6. Limitation of liability
To the maximum extent permitted by law, Provider's total liability arising out of or related to this Agreement or any Deliverable is limited to **the fees paid by Client for the specific Deliverable** giving rise to the claim. Provider is not liable for lost profits, losses on awarded contracts, or indirect, incidental or consequential damages.

## 7. Payment
Monthly plans are billed in advance on the [1st] of each month. Single takeoffs are billed on delivery. Invoices are due within [7] days. Provider may pause work on overdue accounts.

## 8. Confidentiality
Provider will keep Client's pricing, estimates, documents and bid results confidential and will not share them with any third party, including other clients, except as needed to perform the Services (e.g. software vendors under confidentiality terms).

## 9. Term and cancellation
Monthly plans renew month-to-month. Either party may cancel with 30 days' written notice.

## 10. Independent contractor
Provider is an independent contractor and not an employee, agent or partner of Client.

## 11. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of [state].
