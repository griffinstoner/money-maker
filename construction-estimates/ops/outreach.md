# Outreach Playbook

**The approach:** don't pitch. Show them work on a job they're bidding right now.

## Where to find prospects (free)

1. **Public plan holders' lists.** City, county, school district, university and state DOT bid portals often list who downloaded the plans. Every sub on that list is bidding a job **right now**.
2. **GC bid invitations.** Many GCs post "subcontractor bids requested" with the trade packages listed.
3. **Supply houses.** The local drywall/paint supply counter knows every sub in town. Ask who's growing and who's always behind.
4. **Trade groups.** Local ABC/AGC chapters, regional drywall/painting contractor associations, and contractor Facebook groups.
5. **Google Maps.** "[trade] contractor [city]". Check their website for "commercial" work.

Log everyone in `pipeline-tracker.csv`.

### South Carolina sources

- **SCBO, South Carolina Business Opportunities** (scbo.sc.gov): the state's official bid ad listing. Filter to Construction and skip road/utility work (that's mostly SCDOT). Look for school, university, county and city building projects. Municipalities, counties and school districts advertise there too.
- **School district procurement pages:** Greenville County Schools, Charleston County School District, Richland One / Richland Two, Horry County Schools, Berkeley County, Spartanburg districts. School renovations are steady painting/flooring work.
- **Universities:** USC, Clemson, College of Charleston and Winthrop. Their facilities departments post capital project bids.
- **GC portals:** big SC general contractors run invites through BuildingConnected. Sign up there for free as an estimating service to see who's bidding.
- **Supply houses:** local paint stores (Sherwin-Williams commercial branches) and drywall suppliers in each metro know which subs are busy.
- **Groups:** ABC Carolinas, AGC of South Carolina, Home Builders Associations (Greenville, Charleston, Columbia).

## Weekly targets

| Activity | Target |
|---|---|
| New prospects added | 25 |
| Personalized first messages | 20 |
| Follow-ups sent | all due |
| Sample takeoffs sent | 2–3 |
| Calls | 2+ |
| **Goal** | 1 retainer every 2–4 weeks |

**Fix the first number that's off:** no replies → messages aren't specific enough. Replies but no calls → the offer is unclear. Calls but no closes → price or trust, so offer the free takeoff.

## Message 1: First touch (email or LinkedIn)

> **Subject:** [Project name] drywall takeoff
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Saw [Company] on the plan holders' list for [Project], bids due [date]. I did a quick takeoff of Level 1 to show you how I work: about **[18,400] SF of 5/8" Type X** and **[4,210] LF of studs**, marked-up sheet attached.
>
> I run a remote estimating service for [drywall] subs: you send plans, I send back a full takeoff priced on your numbers within 48 hours. Happy to do the rest of [Project] free so you can check it against yours.
>
> Want it?
>
> [Your name]
> [Phone] · [website]

**Before sending:** the partial takeoff must be real and checked. Never send made-up numbers.

## Message 2: Follow-up (3 business days later)

> Hi [Name], following up on [Project]. Bids are due [date]. The offer to do the full takeoff free still stands if it would help. Just reply "yes" and send the link to your plans.

## Message 3: Final follow-up (7 days later)

> Hi [Name], last note from me. If takeoffs ever take up your evenings, I can take that off your plate: 48-hour turnaround, priced on your numbers, $1,500/mo for up to 6 jobs. First one's free whenever you want to try it.

## Reply to "How much?"

> Two options:
> - **Per job:** $250–900 depending on size. I quote before I start.
> - **Bid Desk:** $1,500/month for up to 6 takeoffs, 48-hour turnaround, scope sheet and GC questions included. That's less than a part-time estimator, with no hiring.
>
> Most people start with a free takeoff on a live bid and compare it with their own. Want to send one over?

## Reply to "We do our own estimating"

> Makes sense. Most of my clients did too. They use me for the overflow, the weeks when 3 bids land at once, so they don't have to skip jobs. Worth having in your back pocket? First one's free.

## Phone call script (10 min)

1. "How many bids do you send a month? How many do you skip because there's no time?"
2. "Who does the takeoffs now? How long does one take?"
3. "What's your win rate, roughly?"
4. Do the math with them: *skipped bids × win rate × average job profit* = money left on the table. Compare that with $1,500.
5. "Send me your next bid and your estimate template. I'll have it back in 48 hours, free."

## Rules

- Every first message must name a **real project they're bidding**.
- No mass email blasts or purchased lists. Follow CAN-SPAM: real name, physical address, and stop when someone asks.
- Never claim experience, clients or results you don't have.
